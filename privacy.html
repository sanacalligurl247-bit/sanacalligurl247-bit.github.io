Privacy Policy

This page describes the personal n8n automation used by RingBack Co., a sole proprietorship, to log its own missed-call leads and send the owner notifications.

What data is accessed

The automation connects, with the owner's authorization, to the owner's own Google Sheets and Gmail accounts. It reads and writes lead-log rows in the owner's own spreadsheet and sends notification emails to the owner. It does not access any other person's Google account or data.

How the data is used

Data is used only to operate the automation described above. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

Google API Limited Use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and security

Lead logs are kept in the owner's own Google account. Access is protected by the owner's Google account credentials and n8n's credential storage on the owner's own computer.
