Eve vs voicemail

Voicemail costs nothing — and that is exactly the problem. The math below isn’t about what voicemail charges. It’s about what it catches, and what walks away while your greeting is still playing.

Worked example — your mileage will vary

Say your shop misses 10 calls a week (about 43 a month). At voicemail’s roughly 15% catch rate, about 6–7 callers a month leave a message you can act on. The other ~36 mostly don’t call back.

With Eve, those ~43 callers get a text within about a minute and are asked what they need, where they are, and when they need it. How many become jobs depends on your close rate — run your own numbers in the missed-call calculator on the homepage.

Fairness note — where voicemail genuinely wins

Voicemail is free, already set up, and needs zero change. If nearly all your callers are existing customers who know you and will wait for a callback — a small, loyal book of repeat work — voicemail may honestly be enough. It also never misunderstands a caller: a rambling message from a longtime customer can carry context a structured text conversation won’t. Eve wins on speed and catch rate for new callers shopping around; voicemail wins on costing nothing and changing nothing.
