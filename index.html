Questions every owner asks

I’m not on Jobber or Housecall Pro. Can I still be bookable?

Yes — that’s exactly who Eve is for. The big booking platforms are one rail. Eve is the other: she answers your missed calls by text, qualifies the job, and books it. A shop with no website and five reviews can be just as bookable as a franchise — it just needs a working way to finish the booking.

Do you guarantee Google will pick my shop?

No — and you should walk away from anyone who does. RingBack guarantees only its deliverables: the audit delivered, the fixes done, and Eve’s line live and answering. We never guarantee rankings, how often an AI mentions you, or how many jobs you get — AI recommendations are controlled by the AI companies, not by us. What we stand behind is the verifiable work: a clean profile, a booking path that ends in a booking, reviews coming in steady. That’s the material the AI picks from.

What does the free check actually get me?

A one-page rundown, by email, answering the questions that matter: does Google’s AI name your shop for your trade in your town, can the customer finish booking — or does the trail die at a phone number nobody answered — and one ChatGPT line: does a business profile exist for your shop, and is it claimed? No call, no obligation. If the answer is “you’re fine,” the page says that too.

What’s the ChatGPT profile check — and does claiming it rank me higher?

No — claiming is not a ranking lever, and we don’t sell it as one. What it buys is accuracy, control, and insights: your profile says what you say it says. The full breakdown is in the ChatGPT check section above.

How much of my time does any of this take?

The check: none — it arrives by email. Foundation: you send the owner-only details by email, we build from them, and you approve the finished setup by email before anything goes live. No meeting, no call on the calendar. Found runs in the background — the only thing that lands on you is Eve’s lead summaries, and those are the good kind of interruption.

I already have a website. Isn’t that enough?

A website used to be the destination. In an AI answer, it’s a footnote — the customer books inside the answer or moves to the next name. Your website still matters as proof you’re real, but the jobs are decided one step earlier now: named, or not; bookable, or not.
