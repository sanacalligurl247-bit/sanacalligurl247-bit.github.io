How much does it cost?

Run the free audit. Your results show the exact price for your business, before you pay a dime. Why not a price list here? Because the work depends on what the audit finds — a business with a clean profile in a quiet town needs far less than one starting from invisible in a competitive trade, and a flat list would overcharge the first and shortchange the second.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend me?

Nobody honest can guarantee what ChatGPT says — anyone who promises that is selling you something. What we guarantee is the work: the checks run, the fixes done, and a fresh rundown every month showing exactly where you stand. Movement is measured, not promised.

Never. Google access is granted through a manager invite you control and can revoke any time. No passwords, no shared logins.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long contracts. Monthly service is month-to-month — pause or cancel with short notice. And your website, profiles, and reviews stay yours if you leave. Everything we set up is in your name, not ours.

How fast do I get my audit?

We run the full 90 checks — not a 30-second automated scan — and email your one-pager when it’s done. You’ll also get the free checklist instantly, right on your screen, while you wait.
