How Text Message Opt-In Works — RingBack Co.

RingBack Co. sends text messages only to people who have opted in, in one of the two ways described on this page. Both are initiated by the end user. RingBack Co. never uses purchased or third-party lists.

1. Keyword opt-in on our website

Our homepage (https://sanacalligurl247-bit.github.io/) displays a section titled "Text us — try the demo," which states:

Text TEXTBACK to (805) 765-3761 to start a demo conversation with Eve, the AI front desk for RingBack Co.

Directly beneath it, the homepage states: "By texting TEXTBACK you agree to receive conversational text messages from RingBack Co. about our missed-call textback service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase."

A person who texts TEXTBACK to (805) 765-3761 has opted in, and the demo conversation begins.

2. Missed-call textback for client businesses

RingBack Co. provides missed-call textback to local businesses. Every client business uses a voicemail greeting that discloses the textback before any text is sent. The greeting script is:

"Thanks for calling [business name]. If we can't get to the phone, we'll send you a quick text from this number so we can help you out. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to that text to opt out."

When a caller reaches no answer, they receive one text from that business's RingBack-powered number. The first text identifies the business, notes that it is replying to their call, and offers STOP to opt out. The standard first text is:

"Hi, this is [business name] — sorry we missed your call! Need a quick answer? Ask me anything — or tell me what you need done and I'll get it started. Reply STOP to end texts."

The conversation continues only if the caller replies. Replying STOP at any point ends the messages.
